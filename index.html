01 / TECHNOLOGY

Integrity built into every frame.

Cryptographic verification and visual forensic analysis work together to make video evidence easier to inspect and audit.

⌗

Frame fingerprinting

Each decoded video frame is converted into a SHA-256 digest. A change to the frame data produces a different digest.

⛓

Tamper-evident chain

Frame digests are linked with their sequence positions and previous chain values, so edits and reordering can be detected against a trusted record.

⌕

Forensic inspection

Verification tools inspect frame mismatches, sequence changes, masking indicators and visual differences for further review.

02 / VERIFICATION FLOW

From video to verifiable record.

Use the forensic workspace to create a reference record, inspect its chain and compare later evidence against the stored baseline.

Ingest video

Choose a recorded video or supported camera source for processing.

Hash each frame

Compute a SHA-256 digest from each decoded frame buffer.

Link the chain

Bind each digest to its predecessor and frame sequence index.

Verify evidence

Compare the evidence with its trusted baseline and inspect reported differences.
